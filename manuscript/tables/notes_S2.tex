All final validation metrics use the same timepoint-stratified Walesky validation split of 1,318 cells, or the explicitly listed subset of that split. Binary labels are defined by normalized Monocle pseudotime > 0.10. Columns labeled high-risk refer to this elevated-pseudotime class, not observed future adverse outcomes. AUROC and AUPRC are dimensionless; AUPRC is average precision. Spearman rho measures association with continuous normalized pseudotime. The primary model was selected using validation AUROC; GNN ablations were selected on an inner development set. This checkpoint-selection asymmetry favors the primary model. The internal comparison is transductive: validation cells remain in the preconstructed graphs and their reference pseudotime values enter transition-pair features. All comparators share expression-derived features and targets. GNN, graph neural network; CI, confidence interval; pp, percentage points; RF, Random Forest; CV, cross-validation; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; AUPRC, area under the precision--recall curve.
