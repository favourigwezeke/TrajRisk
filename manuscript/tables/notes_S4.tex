The shared scVI representation contains 6,582 Walesky mouse hepatocytes and 49,984 Matchett human hepatocyte nuclei on 13,122 retained unique mouse--human gene-symbol pairs, with 30 latent dimensions. The cross-species GNN and seeder were trained on mouse cells in these shared coordinates; human donor labels were not used for model training. \SelfOnly{} is the primary human analysis and Reference-KNN is a sensitivity analysis. APAP-ALF, acetaminophen-induced acute liver failure; ASW, average silhouette width; GNN, graph neural network; KNN, k-nearest neighbors; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
