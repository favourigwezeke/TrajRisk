The Walesky-trained mouse model and comparators were transferred without refitting. \SelfOnly{} inference uses the learned self pathway without incoming reference edges. Reference-KNN inference adds the 15 nearest Walesky reference neighbors in the integrated 30-dimensional scVI space. AUROC CIs use 1,000 bootstrap resamples. Random AUPRC equals the positive-class prevalence. Reported fractions and model scores are dimensionless. APAP, acetaminophen; KNN, k-nearest neighbors; CI, confidence interval; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; AUPRC, area under the precision--recall curve.
