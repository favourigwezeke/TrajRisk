Raw RF probabilities below 0.30 were discarded before row normalization; when no target met this threshold, the 15 highest-probability targets were retained. Normalized probabilities below 0.001 were subsequently removed as a final sparsification step. Full-graph adjacency sparsity was calculated relative to the complete 6,582 \(\times\) 6,582 cell graph. Transition entropy is Shannon entropy of outgoing transition probabilities in nats, summarized across source cells as mean \(\pm\) standard deviation. RF--Gaussian Pearson r denotes the mean row-wise correlation between learned RF transitions and distance-only Gaussian transitions. Elapsed time is in hours; training-pair and edge counts are integers. RF, Random Forest; CV, cross-validation; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
