Spatial scores are mean counts per million (CPM) across signature genes after spot-level library-size normalization. These are gene-signature scores, rather than spatially projected GNN score predictions. The risk signature contains 50 genes. Matchett pericentral spots have \textit{Cyp2e1}/\allowbreak{}\textit{CYP2E1} CPM at or above the within-section 75th percentile; periportal spots are at or below the 25th percentile; remaining spots are midlobular. Percentiles use linear interpolation, and all boundary ties enter the corresponding zone. \textit{Cyp2e1}/\allowbreak{}\textit{CYP2E1} is excluded from Matchett recovery and net scoring, leaving 49 recovery genes, to avoid circularity with zonation. P values are nominal and spot-pooled tests do not use donors as independent inferential units. APAP-ALF, acetaminophen-induced acute liver failure; CPM, counts per million; GNN, graph neural network; SD, standard deviation.
